## What is Neuralchemy Labs?

Neuralchemy Labs is the experimental division of Neuralchemy.  
It focuses on early-stage ideas, research prototypes, and fast technical experiments.

Projects here are not always stable — they exist to:
- Test new ideas  
- Explore new directions  
- Validate assumptions  
- Discover what is worth building long-term  

---





## How Labs Works

Neuralchemy Labs follows a simple loop:

1. Build fast  
2. Test early  
3. Learn in public  
4. Iterate or discard  

Not every project survives.  
The goal is learning and discovery, not perfection.
